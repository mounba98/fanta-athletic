## 🎬 PRODUCTION NOTES

### Strumenti Consigliati
- **Editing**: Adobe Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve
- **Motion Graphics**: After Effects
- **Screen Recording**: OBS Studio / ScreenFlow
- **Voiceover**: Audacity / Adobe Audition
- **Music**: Epidemic Sound / Artlist

### Best Practices
1. **60fps** per animazioni smooth
2. **1080p** minimo, 4K preferito
3. **Captions/sottotitoli** sempre (80% utenti guardano senza audio)
4. **Aspect ratios**: 16:9 (YouTube), 1:1 (IG/FB), 9:16 (Stories)
5. **Brand consistency**: Logo sempre visibile
6. **Call-out visual**: Highlight URL con arrow/box
